The Best Movies According to Sue

My Top 3 Movies of All-Time.

Doctor Strange (2016)

I was amazed by the visual effects and the unique take on the superhero genre. I even tried to replicate some of the spells using Adobe After Effects!

Project Hail Mary (2026)

I'm not a Sci-Fi fan, but this movie was a surprise hit! Aside the Sci-fi part, there was something deep and meaningful about it.

Spirited Away (2001)

My favourite animated film of all time! I was even inspired to study animation after watching it.
